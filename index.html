Privacy Policy

Floating Eco Bottle · Last updated: October 31, 2026 · 中文版见下方

Floating Eco Bottle (“the game”) is a single-player offline game. This page explains exactly what data the game handles, in plain language.

1. What we store

  • Game progress — nutrients, creatures, bottles, names you give your pets, daily tasks and settings. This is stored only on your device (Unity PlayerPrefs). We operate no servers and never upload your save.
  • Anonymous usage counters — session count, which screens were opened, how many ads were watched. These stay on your device as well. They exist so we can understand where players get stuck. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Usage Data, which also erases what was collected.
  • Diagnostic text — the most recent error messages, kept on your device only, so you can copy them if you contact us about a bug.

2. Advertising

  • The game shows rewarded video ads only (never forced interstitials) through Google AdMob (Google LLC).
  • AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate region and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Depending on your region and your consent choices, this may be used for personalised advertising.
  • You can change your consent choices at any time in Settings → Privacy Options. Buying Remove Ads stops all ad requests.
  • Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a product was purchased, which we record locally so your items stay unlocked.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ad requests are tagged accordingly and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Your rights

  • You may withdraw ad consent or change privacy options from the in-game settings.
  • You may reset your advertising identifier in your device system settings.
  • Because the game keeps no server-side data, deleting the app deletes everything we would otherwise hold.

6. Contact

Questions or data requests: huangzhaoling56usa@gmail.com

隐私政策（中文）

《浮游生态瓶》是一款单机离线游戏。本页说明游戏实际处理哪些数据。

1. 我们保存什么

  • 游戏进度：养分、生物、瓶子、你给生物取的名字、每日任务与设置。 这些只保存在你的设备上，我们没有任何服务器，不会上传你的存档。
  • 匿名使用计数：会话次数、打开过哪些界面、看了几次广告。 同样只保存在本机，用于判断玩家卡在哪一步。可随时在 设置 → Usage Data 关闭，关闭会同时清除已记录的内容。
  • 诊断文本：最近几条错误信息，仅存本机，方便你反馈问题时复制给我们。

2. 广告

  • 游戏内只有激励视频（没有强制插屏），由 Google AdMob 提供。
  • AdMob 可能收集设备标识符、大致地区与广告互动数据，用于投放与统计广告；视你所在 地区与同意选择，可能用于个性化广告。
  • 你可以在 设置 → Privacy Options 中随时修改同意选择；购买「去广告」后 不再发起任何广告请求。

3. 内购

内购由 App Store / Google Play 全程处理，我们看不到也不保存你的支付信息，只会收到 「已购买」的结果并在本机记录，以便道具保持解锁。

4. 儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童，广告请求已按此标注，我们不会有意收集儿童数据。

5. 你的权利

  • 可随时在游戏设置中撤回广告同意或修改隐私选项。
  • 可在系统设置中重置广告标识符。
  • 由于没有任何服务端数据，卸载游戏即等于删除我们可能持有的全部信息。

6. 联系方式

问题或数据请求： huangzhaoling56usa@gmail.com
